Product Designer

Description

A product designer creates digital products like apps and websites. They combine user needs with business goals to design experiences that are both intuitive and effective. They are responsible for the entire design process, from concept to final visuals.

Requirements

The core requirements for a product designer typically include proven skills in user-centered design, wireframing, and prototyping, along with proficiency in tools like Figma. A strong portfolio demonstrating problem-solving and end-to-end design process is essential. Most roles require practical experience and the ability to collaborate effectively with product managers and engineers.
