What is Campus GIG?

SMMS (Safeguarding Mobile Money Services) is a centralized and transparent platform that enables real-time verification of phone number statuses (active, inactive, reassigned) to reduce fraud, enhance trust, and improve digital service delivery across sectors.

Who can use this platform?

This platform is only for verified students of the school. You'll need your school email and student ID to sign up.

How do I get verified?

Sign up with your school email and submit your student ID. Once verified, you can apply for jobs.

How do I apply for a job?

Open a job, review the details, and submit a short proposal explaining why you're a good fit.

Can I apply for multiple jobs at once?

Yes, you can apply to as many jobs as you want as long as you meet the requirements.

How do I know if a client selected me?

You'll receive a message and see the job status update in your dashboard.

How do I get paid?

Once your work is approved, your payment is sent to your wallet.

When can I withdraw my money?

You can transfer funds from your wallet to your payout account at any time.

When does a job officially start?

A job starts once both you and the client have signed the contract.
